## 🏴‍☠️ Emulators

Play retro games from any era legally (dump your own cartridges/discs).

- **🏆 [RetroArch](https://www.retroarch.com/)** -All-in-one emulator frontend. Runs cores for almost every retro system ever made. Free and open source. Available on PC, Android, and more.

- **💎 [Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org/)** -GameCube and Wii emulator. Outstanding accuracy and performance. Open source.

- **💎 [RPCS3](https://rpcs3.net/)** -PlayStation 3 emulator. Remarkable progress -most games are playable. Open source.

- **💎 [PCSX2](https://pcsx2.net/)** -PlayStation 2 emulator. Very mature, excellent compatibility. Open source.

- **💎 [Ryujinx](https://ryujinx.org/)** -Nintendo Switch emulator. Good compatibility, active development. Open source.

- **🔗 [PPSSPP](https://www.ppsspp.org/)** -PSP emulator. Works great on PC and Android. Open source.

- **🔗 [Cemu](https://cemu.info/)** -Wii U emulator. Now open source. Breath of the Wild runs beautifully.

- **🔗 [DeSmuME](https://desmume.org/)** -Nintendo DS emulator. Solid and reliable.

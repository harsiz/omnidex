# Gaming

Free and paid platforms, tools, and services for PC, console, and mobile gaming.

:::info Trust Rating Emojis

    **"💎"** indicates recommended and trusted source.
    **"🏆"** indicates the best pick in the author(s) opinion.
:::

---

## 🛒 PC Game Stores

Where to buy and download PC games.

- **🏆 [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/)** — The dominant PC gaming platform. Massive library, frequent sales, Workshop mod support, cloud saves. Free to download.

- **💎 [GOG](https://www.gog.com/)** — DRM-free games. You own what you buy, no launcher required. Strong catalogue of classics and indie titles. Run by CD Projekt.

- **💎 [Itch.io](https://itch.io/)** — Open marketplace for indie games. Thousands of free games, pay-what-you-want bundles, very dev-friendly. FOSS-adjacent spirit.

- **🔗 [Epic Games Store](https://store.epicgames.com/)** — Gives away free games every week. Smaller library than Steam but worth checking for free picks.

- **🔗 [Humble Bundle](https://www.humblebundle.com/)** — Pay-what-you-want game bundles. Portion of proceeds go to charity. Steam keys included on most bundles.

- **🔗 [Fanatical](https://www.fanatical.com/)** — Discounted Steam keys and bundles. Good for finding cheap games.

## 🆓 Free-to-Play

Legitimately free games worth your time.

- **🏆 [Warframe](https://www.warframe.com/)** — Free-to-play co-op action game. Exceptionally generous monetisation model. Available on PC, PS5, Xbox, Switch.

- **💎 [Path of Exile 2](https://www.pathofexile.com/)** — Deep free-to-play ARPG. No pay-to-win. Cosmetic-only store.

- **💎 [Fortnite](https://www.fortnite.com/)** — Free battle royale. Massive player base, constant updates. PC, console, mobile.

- **💎 [Genshin Impact](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/)** — Free open-world RPG. High production value. Gacha monetisation — fun without spending.

- **🔗 [Destiny 2](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/Destiny/NewLight)** — Free-to-play base game. Solid FPS with RPG elements. Some content requires purchase.

- **🔗 [League of Legends](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/)** — The most-played PC game in the world. Free, cosmetic-only store.

- **🔗 [Valorant](https://playvalorant.com/)** — Tactical FPS from Riot Games. Free to play, cosmetic-only purchases.

## 🎮 Game Subscription Services

Pay once, play hundreds of games.

- **🏆 [Xbox Game Pass](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-game-pass)** — Best value in gaming subscriptions. Hundreds of games including day-one first-party releases. PC and console. ~$9.99-14.99/mo.

- **💎 [EA Play](https://www.ea.com/ea-play)** — Access to EA's back catalogue. FIFA, Battlefield, Mass Effect, etc. ~$4.99/mo. Included in Game Pass Ultimate.

- **💎 [PlayStation Plus](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/ps-plus/)** — Monthly free games + online multiplayer for PS4/PS5. Extra/Premium tiers add game catalogue access.

- **🔗 [Ubisoft+](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/ubisoftplus)** — Access to Ubisoft's full catalogue. Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, Rainbow Six, etc. ~$17.99/mo.

## 🏴‍☠️ Emulation

Play retro games from any era legally (dump your own cartridges/discs).

- **🏆 [RetroArch](https://www.retroarch.com/)** — All-in-one emulator frontend. Runs cores for almost every retro system ever made. Free and open source. Available on PC, Android, and more.

- **💎 [Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org/)** — GameCube and Wii emulator. Outstanding accuracy and performance. Open source.

- **💎 [RPCS3](https://rpcs3.net/)** — PlayStation 3 emulator. Remarkable progress — most games are playable. Open source.

- **💎 [PCSX2](https://pcsx2.net/)** — PlayStation 2 emulator. Very mature, excellent compatibility. Open source.

- **💎 [Ryujinx](https://ryujinx.org/)** — Nintendo Switch emulator. Good compatibility, active development. Open source.

- **🔗 [PPSSPP](https://www.ppsspp.org/)** — PSP emulator. Works great on PC and Android. Open source.

- **🔗 [Cemu](https://cemu.info/)** — Wii U emulator. Now open source. Breath of the Wild runs beautifully.

- **🔗 [DeSmuME](https://desmume.org/)** — Nintendo DS emulator. Solid and reliable.

## 🚀 Game Launchers

Unified launchers to manage all your games in one place.

- **🏆 [Playnite](https://playnite.link/)** — Open source game launcher that unifies Steam, GOG, Epic, emulators and more into one place. Highly customisable with extensions.

- **💎 [Hydra](https://github.com/hydralauncher/hydra)** — Game launcher with built-in torrent client and achievement support. Open source.

- **💎 [Heroic Games Launcher](https://heroicgameslauncher.com/)** — Open source launcher for Epic and GOG games. Great for Linux and Steam Deck users.

- **🔗 [GOG Galaxy](https://www.gog.com/galaxy)** — GOG's official launcher. Can also import and track games from other platforms.

- **🔗 [LaunchBox](https://www.launchbox-app.com/)** — Retro and console game library organiser with rich metadata and artwork. Great for emulation setups.

## 🔧 Optimization Tools

Get more performance and control out of your games.

- **🏆 [PCGamingWiki](https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/)** — Fixes, tweaks, and workarounds for PC game issues. If a game is broken, check here first.

- **💎 [MSI Afterburner](https://www.msi.com/Landing/afterburner)** — GPU overclocking and fan control. Overlays in-game FPS, temps, and usage. Free. (Skip Norton 360 in the installer.)

- **💎 [SpecialK](https://www.special-k.info/)** — Advanced per-game tweaking tool. HDR, frame pacing, texture injection, and more. Free.

- **💎 [ReShade](https://reshade.me/)** — Generic post-processing injector. Adds shaders, HDR, SSAO, and visual effects to almost any game.

- **💎 [DLSS Swapper](https://github.com/beeradmoore/dlss-swapper)** — Download, manage, and swap DLSS versions in your games without waiting for patches.

- **🔗 [Lossless Scaling](https://store.steampowered.com/app/993090/Lossless_Scaling/)** — Frame generation and upscaling for any game. Paid, but very effective.

- **🔗 [dgVoodoo2](https://dege.freeweb.hu/dgVoodoo2/)** — Glide and DirectX wrapper for running old games on modern hardware.

- **🔗 [4GB Patch](https://ntcore.com/?page_id=371)** — Patches 32-bit game executables to use up to 4GB of RAM instead of 2GB.

- **🔗 [Windowed Borderless Gaming](https://github.com/Codeusa/Borderless-Gaming)** — Forces any game to run in a borderless window. Open source.

- **🔗 [Flawless Widescreen](https://www.flawlesswidescreen.org/)** — Fixes FOV and aspect ratio issues for ultrawide and multi-monitor gaming.

## 🎮 Controller Tools

- **🏆 [DS4Windows](https://ds4-windows.com/)** — Use PlayStation controllers on PC with full feature support including touchpad and gyro.

- **💎 [AntiMicroX](https://github.com/AntiMicroX/antimicrox)** — Map gamepad buttons to keyboard/mouse inputs. Open source.

- **💎 [x360ce](https://www.x360ce.com/)** — Emulates an Xbox 360 controller for games that don't support DirectInput. Open source.

- **🔗 [JoyToKey](https://joytokey.net/)** — Map controller inputs to keyboard and mouse. Lightweight and reliable.

- **🔗 [Gamepad Tester](https://gamepad-tester.com/)** — Quick browser-based controller input tester. No install needed.

## 🧩 Mods

- **🏆 [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/)** — The largest modding platform. Thousands of mods for hundreds of games. Free account available.

- **💎 [ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/)** — Long-running mod hosting. Great for older games and total conversion mods.

- **💎 [GameBanana](https://gamebanana.com/)** — Community mods focused on fighting games, Source engine games, and more.

- **💎 [Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/)** — Mod platform for Unity games. Use with [r2modman](https://github.com/ebkr/r2modmanPlus) for easy install.

- **🔗 [Mod.io](https://mod.io/)** — Cross-platform mod support API used by many games natively.

- **🔗 [WabbаJack](https://www.wabbajack.org/)** — Automated modlist installer for Bethesda games. One-click mod packs.

## 🎯 Aim Training

- **🔗 [3D Aim Trainer](https://www.3daimtrainer.com/)** — Browser-based aim trainer. No download needed.

## 📊 Stats & Tracking

- **🏆 [IsThereAnyDeal](https://isthereanydeal.com/)** — Track game prices across all stores. Set price alerts. Never pay full price again.

- **💎 [Backloggd](https://www.backloggd.com/)** — Track games you've played, want to play, and write reviews. Like Letterboxd for games.

- **💎 [ProtonDB](https://www.protondb.com/)** — Community reports on how well Windows games run on Linux via Steam Proton. Essential for Steam Deck users.

- **🔗 [SteamDB](https://www.steamdb.info/)** — Detailed Steam stats, historical pricing, player counts, and sale history.

- **🔗 [Speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/)** — Speedrunning leaderboards, streams, and resources for thousands of games.

- **🔗 [PSNProfiles](https://psnprofiles.com/)** — Trophy guides and PSN profile viewer for PlayStation players.

## 🎙️ Voice & Communities

- **🏆 [Discord](https://discord.com/)** — The go-to platform for gaming communities. Voice, text, video. Free. Servers for almost every game imaginable.

- **💎 [Mumble](https://www.mumble.info/)** — Lightweight, low-latency open source voice chat. Self-hostable. Better voice quality than Discord for competitive play.

- **🔗 [TeamSpeak](https://www.teamspeak.com/)** — Long-standing gaming voice client. Still used by some competitive communities.

## 📡 Remote Play & Streaming

- **🏆 [Sunshine](https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine) + [Moonlight](https://moonlight-stream.org/)** — Open source self-hosted game streaming. Stream from your PC to any device on your network (or remotely). Free.

- **💎 [Parsec](https://parsec.app/)** — Low-latency remote desktop built for gaming. Great for playing with friends on the same PC remotely.

- **🔗 [GeForce NOW](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce-now/)** — Stream your existing Steam/Epic library from NVIDIA's servers. Free tier available.

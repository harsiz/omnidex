# Privacy

According to many sources, privacy is a **human right**.. but it seems to us that many websites and services don't actually adopt that by default, and require you to do manual work just to protect yourself from being constantly monitored by your **[Internet Service Provider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_service_provider)**.

Below, here is a good list to protect yourself from tracking trolls when surfing the web, downloading apps and **[FOSS](/foss)** or just using your Operating System.

<br/>

:::info Trust Rating Emojis

    **"💎"** indicates recommended and trusted source.
    **"🏆"** indicates the best pick in the author(s) opinion.
:::

## AdBlocking

When surfing the web, adblockers are a fundamental MUST.
>Why?

Advertisements aren't inherently bad, but the way companies go about them to get the most accurate demographic to sell to advertisers possible are by blatant fingerprinting and tracking of you while you browse.

A simple solution to that?

An **AdBlocker**.

<br/>

- **🏆 [uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/)** - The best content blocking system out to date and for good reason.

    Not only does it come with:
    - content blocking
    - pop-up blocking
    - DNS blocking for multiple devices
    - native firefox support
    
    but it works pretty much off the bat **absolutely everywhere.**

- **🏆 [Adblock Plus](https://adblockplus.org/)** - The world's "number 1" free ad-blocker. It is free and is one of the most downloaded extensions on the Google Chrome webstore.
    It doesn't do much but block ads, and there is a paid plan (but the free plan does just as well for 90% of its user-base).

- **🏆 [Stands Adblocker](https://www.standsapp.org/)** - just another simple ad-blocking extension that works on modern Chromium browsers. It gets the job done, without fluff.

- **🏆 [SponsorBlock](https://sponsor.ajay.app/)** - Skips sponsored Youtube Ads. It uses Natural Language Processing to determined when videos are sponsored and when they end the sponsored segment. 80% of the time it works without flaws. Such a life saver when you need information fast and you don't want to be bombarded with advertisements.

- **💎 [Disblock Origin](https://codeberg.org/AllPurposeMat/Disblock-Origin)** - Block discord _"nitro"_ CTAs and pop-ups indefinitely. 

## Spending / Receiving Money Private

When sending and receiving money online, you'd potentially not want to have a bank or overseer monitoring all your transactions, especially if the transactions are particularly large...

It's a good thing that in the modern digital age, we have many solutions to keep are transactions more private than currently.

<br/>

>Why not just pay with Credit / Debit Cards?

You can.. but that's the most easiest to be tracked by far. 

Every transaction has to be overseen and verified by banks and payment providers who can not only monitor your transactions, but censor your transactions without notice.

**So Here are some good options when paying privately..**

- **🏆 'Paying in Giftcards'** - Giftcards are a good choice for anonymity because it shields the point of **PII** (Personally Identifiable Information) by acting as the "service-only debit card". It doesn't require you to put in your details (depending on where you're getting them from) and is pretty easy to get.

- **🏆 '[Cryptocurrency](/privacy#cryptocurrency)'** - Crypto is an amazing way to pay for digital services online without relying on a bank or intermediary (like Visa/Mastercard) to process transactions on behalf of you. Infact, we will go deeper into the usage of cryptocurrency for handling transactions in a second.

- **💎 'Phone NFC Pay (Apple / Google / Samsung)'** - Although not as anonymous as cryptocurrency or giftcard payments, NFC / device contactless payment is much more secure than traditional payment cards because it encrypts bank card data before shipping it to merchants.

    >_(Not every card supports it though!!)_

- **🔗 '[PayPal](https://paypal.com)'** - PayPal is a "meh". It gets the job done by routing its payment through its infrastructure, which means it doesn't actually show your card details to the merchant. The only problem is that it assumes that you trust PayPal entirely.

## Cryptocurrency

Cryptocurrency is an entirely different species finance-wise. It's the first digital monetary system that truly doesn't rely on a bank or intermediary to manage and settle transactions. Instead of trusting a bank to handle that for you, YOU are your own bank, and the whole narrative of using cryptocurrency is that you aren't required to trust anyone.

There are numerous cryptocurrencies built for solving different tech (much like how there are different **[Linux Distros](/foss.html#operating-systems)** for different use-cases.) 

One major reason to prefer cryptocurrency over traditional finance is that it is intended for borderless payments, that can operate from any country on Earth that has a stable internet connection. No country sanctions can ever take it down **[(and they've tried)...](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legality_of_cryptocurrency_by_country_or_territory)**.

Here are the different recommended cryptocurrencies for when planning on spending your hard earned cash online.

-  **🏆 '[Bitcoin](https://bitcoin.org)'** - The king of cryptocurrency. It is the most widely supported option, although it has a transparent blockchain, for most people, it's as private as using a pseudonym behind a Reddit account.
<br/>

-  **🏆 '[Monero](https://www.getmonero.org/)'** - A crypto coin especially designed for handling private payments. Payments are routed through **[Tor](/foss.html#:~:text=%F0%9F%8F%86%20Tor%20Browser)** or other network protocols such as **[I2P](/internet-tools#i2p)**.
    It is de facto the golden standard when it comes to private and anonymous payments just because it _just gets it right_.

    It uses Stealth one-time addresses to hide the receiving address.

    It uses Ring Confidential Transactions to hide the amount being sent.

    It uses Ring Signatures to hide the sender by jumbling it up with decoy addresses.

- **💎 [USD₮ (USD Tether)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tether_(cryptocurrency))** - This is a stablecoin for people who don't want to rely on the volatility of using **Bitcoin or Monero.**

    It is _slightly_ more reliant on a third party company (Tether) to make sure your 1 USDT token will always equate to one legitimate US Dollar (luckily, they've been doing this for a while and it has remained around the same $1.00 USD mark since its inception).

    BUT it is much more anonymous compared to directly transacting via card as you can use any regular **Ethereum, Solana, Cardano** wallet address to transact with USDT. They also rarely handle fund blacklists / freezing compared to USDC who dish them out freely.

- **🔗 [USDC (USD Coin)](https://www.usdc.com/)** - Another stablecoin that has more regulation. Good for legitimate merchants. Mediocre for privacy.

- **🔗 [Litecoin](https://litecoin.com/)** - Fast settlement compared to Bitcoin while remaining as decentralized.

- **🔗 [Solana](https://solana.com/)** - Lightning fast speed. Transactions processing down to the millisecond. Very VERY scalable and has Visa level potential.. *but* not everyone can run a blockchain due to the computational power necessary.. it sadly falls back into the hands of centralized banking.

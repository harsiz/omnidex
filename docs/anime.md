# Anime

Tons of ways to watch either the latest anime shows, or an old beauty, in amazing quality!

## Streaming Sites

There are numerous ways to truly watch anime online, and depending on the route you'd like to go, you can follow through entirely legal measures.

### Paid / Proprietary Medium

- 🔗 **[Crunchyroll](https://crunchyroll.com)**

    This is the largest _legal_ anime media library available world wide, with the largest library. It requires a VPN to view many of the shows as they are usually geo-blocked (a _'benefit?'_ of being regulatory compliant). 

    Although that is the case, it still remains one of the largest anime streaming services to date, boasting well over **3000** recorded animes.

    **Pros**:
    - Largest anime library for any legal worldwide platform (3000+ anime)
    - Long standing anime platform, has lasted many years

    **Cons:**
    - Paid platform ($5 - 7.99 USD on Standard plans)
    - No community / comment pages.

- 🔗 **[Netflix](https://netflix.com)**

    This website is a useful streaming website which not just contains anime, but western titles aswell. Most people already recognise Netflix so we won't introduce it as if it was a new website entirely. The site has over **50+** top anime titles, with another 150 regular ones.
    
    **Pros:**
    - 4K video playback on all recent anime
    - Low latency (it's the money of Netflix)

    **Cons:**
    - Very small library
    - Paid service (monthly: $12 - 15.99 USD in many countries)
    - Geo-blocked in many areas

- 🔗 **[Hulu](https://hulu.com)**

    This platform is similar to many other streaming services. It boasts a select amount of large anime titles. Hulu's one in particular is astronomically tiny compared to the likes of **Crunchyroll** or **Netflix**. They hold famous titles like _Bleach_... and that's really it.

    **Pros:**
    - They have **Bleach**.

    **Cons:**
    - Limited selection
    - Paid Service

- 🔗 **[Youtube](https://youtube.com)** (in particular, renting shows directly from Youtube.)

    Believe it or not, **Youtube** does infact have a library for anime and other forms of Japanese material. They have entirely full series' with average quality.

    **Pros:**
    - Very convenient (it's on Youtube)
    - Comes with Premium

    **Cons:**
    - Paid (usually have to buy shows or pay via Youtube Premium)
    - Low customisation (can't change quality much, stuck to 1080p / 720p / 480p depending on detected device)
    - Small anime library (most of their sources are just mirrored from **Crunchyroll**)

- 🔗 **[Paramount Plus](https://paramount.com)**

    Paramount Plus is similar to Hulu in terms of selection. It doesn't need a dedicated review on the software. 

    **Pros:**
    - Good video quality (subjective)

    **Cons:**
    - Limited library
    - Not very convenient
    - Geo-blocking episodes

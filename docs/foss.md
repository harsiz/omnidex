# FOSS

**Free and Open Source Software** — software where anyone can view, modify, and distribute the source code. No hidden telemetry, no vendor lock-in, no cost.

:::info Trust Rating Emojis

    **"💎"** indicates recommended and trusted software.
    **"🏆"** indicates the best pick in the author(s) opinion.

All software listed here has been reviewed. Always download from the official sources linked below.
:::

---

## 🌐 Browsers

- **🏆 [Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/firefox/)** — The gold standard for a privacy-respecting, fully open source browser. Highly extensible with uBlock Origin, and not built on Chromium.

- **💎 [Librewolf](https://librewolf.net/)** — A fork of Firefox hardened out of the box. Strips telemetry, enforces strict privacy defaults. No tweaking required.

- **💎 [Ungoogled Chromium](https://github.com/ungoogled-software/ungoogled-chromium)** — Chromium with all Google services and telemetry surgically removed. If you need a Chromium-based browser, this is it.

- **🔗 [Brave](https://brave.com/)** — Chromium-based, built-in ad/tracker blocking. Note: not fully FOSS (some proprietary components in the rewards system), but widely used.

---

## 🔍 Search Engines

- **🏆 [SearXNG](https://searxng.org/)** — A self-hostable, open source metasearch engine. Aggregates results from many engines with zero tracking. Many public instances available.

- **💎 [Brave Search](https://search.brave.com/)** — Independent index (not just Google/Bing). No tracking. Owned by Brave, not fully open source but privacy-respecting.

- **💎 [DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com/)** — Long-standing no-tracking search. Uses Bing results. Not open source, but privacy-focused and beginner-friendly.

- **🔗 [Startpage](https://www.startpage.com/)** — Google results with no tracking. Useful if you want Google quality without Google surveillance.

---

## 📧 Email

- **🏆 [Proton Mail](https://proton.me/mail)** — End-to-end encrypted email. Swiss privacy laws. Free tier available. Open source clients.

- **💎 [Tutanota](https://tuta.com/)** — E2E encrypted, fully open source. Stricter than Proton on FOSS principles. Based in Germany.

- **🔗 [SimpleLogin](https://simplelogin.io/)** — Email alias service. Create unlimited aliases to hide your real address. Open source, now part of Proton.

---

## 💬 Messaging

- **🏆 [Signal](https://signal.org/)** — End-to-end encrypted messaging and calls. Open source. The benchmark for secure communication.

- **💎 [Element / Matrix](https://element.io/)** — Decentralised, federated messaging. Self-hostable. E2E encryption available. Great for communities.

- **🔗 [Session](https://getsession.org/)** — No phone number required. Decentralised, onion-routed. Fully open source.

- **🔗 [Briar](https://briarproject.org/)** — Peer-to-peer encrypted messaging. Works over Tor, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth. No central server.

## 🛡️ VPN

:::warning
Free VPNs are almost always a bad deal — if you're not paying, your data is the product. Use reputable paid options or self-host.
:::

- **🏆 [Mullvad](https://mullvad.net/)** — No accounts, no logs, accepts cash/crypto. Consistently audited. Open source clients. ~5€/month flat.

- **💎 [ProtonVPN](https://protonvpn.com/)** — Open source, audited, no-logs policy. Free tier available (limited servers). From the Proton team.

- **🔗 [IVPN](https://www.ivpn.net/)** — Privacy-focused, audited, anonymous accounts. Open source clients. Slightly pricier but trustworthy.

- **🔗 [WireGuard](https://www.wireguard.com/)** — Not a VPN provider but the open source VPN protocol. If you self-host, use this.

## 🗺️ Maps & Navigation

- **🏆 [OsmAnd](https://osmand.net/)** — Offline maps powered by OpenStreetMap. Full navigation, no Google. Android & iOS.

- **💎 [Organic Maps](https://organicmaps.app/)** — Simpler and faster OsmAnd alternative. Offline-first, no tracking, no ads. Ideal for travel.

- **🔗 [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/)** — The open source map of the world. The data layer behind most FOSS map apps.

## 🖥️ Operating Systems

- **🏆 [Linux Mint](https://linuxmint.com/)** — The most beginner-friendly Linux distro. Stable, polished, great for Windows converts. Cinnamon desktop.

- **💎 [Fedora](https://fedoraproject.org/)** — Cutting-edge but stable. Sponsored by Red Hat. Excellent hardware support.

- **💎 [Debian](https://www.debian.org/)** — Ultra-stable, the base of many other distros (Ubuntu, Mint, etc.). Ideal for servers or reliability-focused setups.

- **💎 [EndeavourOS](https://endeavouros.com/)** — Arch-based, beginner-friendly. Rolling release, minimal bloat, excellent community.

- **🔗 [Tails](https://tails.boum.org/)** — A live OS that routes all traffic through Tor. Leaves no trace. For high-privacy situations.

- **🔗 [Qubes OS](https://www.qubes-os.org/)** — Security through compartmentalisation. Runs apps in isolated VMs. Steep learning curve, extremely secure.

## 📝 Office & Productivity

- **🏆 [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/)** — Full Microsoft Office alternative. Writer, Calc, Impress, Draw, Base. Completely free and open source.

- **💎 [OnlyOffice](https://www.onlyoffice.com/)** — Strong MS Office compatibility. Cleaner UI than LibreOffice. Self-hostable server edition.

- **🔗 [Cryptpad](https://cryptpad.org/)** — Encrypted, collaborative documents, spreadsheets, and more. No account needed.

---

## 🎵 Media Players

- **🏆 [VLC](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/)** — Plays virtually any format. No codecs needed. Open source. The undisputed king of media players.

- **💎 [mpv](https://mpv.io/)** — Lightweight, scriptable, no UI bloat. Preferred by power users. Excellent for high quality video playback.

- **🔗 [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org/)** — Self-hosted media server. Your personal Netflix. Open source alternative to Plex.

---

## 🔐 Password Managers

- **🏆 [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/)** — Open source, cloud-synced, free tier is excellent. Self-hostable (Vaultwarden). Best overall pick.

- **💎 [KeePassXC](https://keepassxc.org/)** — Local-only, no cloud. Encrypted database file you control entirely. For people who don't want any sync.

---

## 🧰 Utilities

- **💎 [7-Zip](https://www.7-zip.org/)** — Open source archive manager. Handles ZIP, RAR, 7z, and dozens more. No bloat.

- **💎 [Ventoy](https://www.ventoy.net/)** — Boot multiple ISOs from one USB drive. Essential for OS installs.

- **💎 [Flameshot](https://flameshot.org/)** — Feature-rich screenshot tool. Annotation, blur, upload. Open source.

- **🔗 [ClamAV](https://www.clamav.net/)** — Open source antivirus. Lighter alternative to commercial AV, community-maintained signatures.

- **🔗 [KDE Connect](https://kdeconnect.kde.org/)** — Seamlessly links your phone and PC. File transfer, notifications, clipboard sync. Open source.

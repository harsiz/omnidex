# Get Started

Here's a guide for the people who are just settling in for the world of _actually_ useful media.

## Well.. why?

There are lots of reasons of why you'd want free and useful tools compared to the conventional routes. Sure, they may be tacky, and take longer to setup, but it is digital sovereignty that you get when using software that fulfills its purpose **WITHOUT** useless bloatware aswell.

So here we choose to provide software that actually serves your purpose, and just does that. Nothing else running under the hood.

So I hope you enjoy them!

## Movies & TV Shows

### Paid / Subscription

The mainstream, paid options.

- **🏆 [Netflix](https://netflix.com)** - Large library and it's trustworthy, but it can be expensive depending on your region and plan. Held back due to a lack of a free tier.

- **🏆 [Amazon Prime Video](https://primevideo.com)** - Lots of originals (The Boys, Reacher), and a wide variety of films. Included with Prime or ~$8.99/mo standalone.

- - **💎 [Apple TV+](https://tv.apple.com)** - Smaller library of titles but originals are consistent in quality (Severance, Ted Lasso). $9.99/mo.

- **🔗 [Disney+](https://disneyplus.com)** - Includes Disney, Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar and National Geographic but not much else.

- **🔗 [Max (HBO Max)](https://max.com)** - ~$9.99–15.99/mo.

- **🔗 [Paramount+](https://paramountplus.com)** - $5.99–11.99/mo.

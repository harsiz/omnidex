# Streaming

Free and paid ways to watch movies, TV shows, and live content — across every device.

:::info Trust Rating Emojis

    **"💎"** indicates recommended and trusted source.
    **"🏆"** indicates the best pick in the author(s) opinion.

## 🎬 Movies & TV Shows

### Paid / Subscription

The mainstream options. Good quality and convenience, but geo-blocking and rotating libraries are common.

- **🔗 [Netflix](https://netflix.com)** — The biggest name in streaming. Large library of originals and licensed content. $12–15.99/mo depending on region.

- **🔗 [Amazon Prime Video](https://primevideo.com)** — Strong originals and licensed titles. Included with Prime, or ~$8.99/mo standalone.

- **🔗 [Disney+](https://disneyplus.com)** — Disney, Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar, National Geographic. Essential if you care about any of those franchises.

- **🔗 [Apple TV+](https://tv.apple.com)** — Smaller library but consistently high quality originals. $9.99/mo, free with new Apple device purchase.

- **🔗 [Max (HBO Max)](https://max.com)** — Home of HBO content. Some of the best TV ever made lives here. ~$9.99–15.99/mo.

- **🔗 [Paramount+](https://paramountplus.com)** — Star Trek, Yellowstone, CBS content. $5.99–11.99/mo.

---

### Free (Ad-Supported)

Legitimate, completely free streaming services supported by ads.

- **🏆 [Tubi](https://tubitv.com)** — Massive free library. Thousands of movies and shows with light ad breaks. No account needed.

- **🏆 [Pluto TV](https://pluto.tv)** — Free live TV channels AND on-demand content. Run by Paramount. No account needed.

- **💎 [Peacock](https://peacocktv.com)** — NBCUniversal's platform. Decent free tier with ads. Includes NBC news, older shows, some originals.

- **💎 [Plex](https://plex.tv)** — Free ad-supported movies and TV, plus a live TV section. Also functions as a personal media server (see below).

- **🔗 [Crackle](https://crackle.com)** — Sony-owned free streaming. Smaller library but legitimate and free.

- **🔗 [Kanopy](https://kanopy.com)** — Free through public libraries and universities. Art house films, documentaries, classics. Check if your library has access.

### Free Sites (Community-Run)

These sites aggregate streams from around the web. Quality and availability varies.

Use an ad blocker. These sites are ad-heavy. [uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/) handles them well.

- **🏆 [Fmovies](https://fmoviesz.to/)** — One of the longest-running free streaming sites. Large library, multiple stream sources.

- **💎 [Lookmovie](https://lookmovie2.to/)** — Clean interface, reliable streams, good for TV series.

- **💎 [Soap2Day](https://soap2day.day/)** — Long-standing site. Wide library. Use with ad blocker.

- **🔗 [Rive](https://rivestream.live/)** — Minimal UI, multiple stream sources per title.

- **🔗 [AZMovies](https://azmovies.ag/)** — Movie-focused, relatively clean UI.

- **🔗 [FlixHQ](https://flixhq.to/)** — Movies and TV, multiple stream sources.

- **🔗 [Vumoo](https://vumoo.to/)** — Straightforward movie streaming, no frills.

## 🏠 Personal Media Servers

Host your own streaming server. Your files, your rules, no subscriptions.

- **🏆 [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org/)** — Fully open source, self-hosted media server. Your personal Netflix. Free forever. Runs on Windows, Linux, Mac, NAS, Docker.

    **Pros:**
    - Completely free and open source
    - No account or internet required after setup
    - Apps for every platform (TV, phone, browser)

    **Cons:**
    - Requires setup and your own hardware
    - You need to source the media yourself

- **💎 [Plex](https://plex.tv/)** — The most polished personal media server. Free with optional Plex Pass for extra features. Easier setup than Jellyfin but some features are paywalled.

- **🔗 [Emby](https://emby.media/)** — Middle ground between Jellyfin and Plex. Free tier available, premium Emby Premiere unlocks more. Note: Jellyfin was forked from Emby when it went closed-source.

- **🔗 [Stremio](https://www.stremio.com/)** — Streaming aggregator with add-on support. Connects to Torrentio and Real-Debrid for a premium feel at low cost.

## 🎙️ Podcasts

- **🏆 [Pocket Casts](https://pocketcasts.com/)** — The best podcast app. Available on all platforms. Free web version, paid app.

- **💎 [AntennaPod](https://antennapod.org/)** — Fully open source podcast player. Android only. No account needed.

- **🔗 [Spotify](https://spotify.com/)** — Massive podcast library alongside music. Free tier available with ads.

- **🔗 [Overcast](https://overcast.fm/)** — iOS-only. Clean, smart speed and voice boost features. Free.

## 🔧 Tools & Add-ons

- **🏆 [uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/)** — Essential for free streaming sites. Blocks ads, pop-ups, and malicious redirects. Available for Firefox and Chromium-based browsers.

- **💎 [Real-Debrid](https://real-debrid.com/)** — Paid unrestricted downloader/stream link resolver (~€3/mo). Works with Stremio + Torrentio for near-Netflix quality streams from open source apps.

- **🔗 [Torrentio](https://torrentio.strem.fun/)** — Stremio add-on that sources streams via torrents. Pair with Real-Debrid for best results.

- **🔗 [Streamlink](https://streamlink.github.io/)** — CLI tool that extracts streams from sites and pipes them into a player like VLC or mpv. Open source.

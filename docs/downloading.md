# Downloading

DDL sites, torrent clients, download managers, debrids, and tools to maximise your download speeds.

:::info Trust Rating Emojis

    **"💎"** indicates recommended and trusted source.
    **"🏆"** indicates the best pick in the author(s) opinion.

Always use an **ad blocker** on DDL and torrent sites. Use a **VPN** when torrenting.
:::

:::warning
Always scan downloaded software with [VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com/) before installing. Never run executables from untrusted sources.
:::

## ⚡ Download Managers

Accelerate downloads, resume broken transfers, and grab files from any site.

- **🏆 [JDownloader 2](https://jdownloader.org/)** — The gold standard free download manager. Handles premium file hosts, CAPTCHA solving, archive extraction, and scheduling. Open source.

- **💎 [Internet Download Manager (IDM)](https://www.internetdownloadmanager.com/)** — Splits downloads into parallel segments for maximum speed. Integrates with all browsers. Paid (~$25 one-time), but widely cracked.

- **💎 [Free Download Manager](https://www.freedownloadmanager.org/)** — Free, open source alternative to IDM. Torrent support built-in, browser integration, fast segmented downloads.

- **💎 [aria2](https://aria2.github.io/)** — Lightweight command-line download utility. Supports HTTP, FTP, BitTorrent, and Metalink. Highly scriptable. Open source.

- **🔗 [Motrix](https://motrix.app/)** — Clean GUI download manager built on aria2. Open source, cross-platform.

- **🔗 [Xtreme Download Manager (XDM)](https://xtremedownloadmanager.com/)** — Open source IDM alternative. Browser integration, video grabber, scheduler.

## 🗂️ DDL Sites (Direct Download)

:::warning
General DDL sites are best for video, audio, books, and magazines. **Avoid them for software and games** — use dedicated sections for those instead.
:::

- **🏆 [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/)** — Non-profit digital library. Video, audio, books, software, ROMs, magazines, and historical documents. Completely free and legal.

- **💎 [SoftArchive](https://sanet.st/)** — Long-running DDL index for audio, books, comics, newspapers, and magazines.

- **💎 [1337x](https://1337x.to/)** — Also has a DDL section alongside torrents. Good for video and audio.

- **🔗 [MaxRelease](https://maxrelease.net/)** — Video, audio, and magazines from scene releases.

- **🔗 [SCNLOG](https://scnlog.me/)** — Scene release log. Video, audio, ROMs, books.

- **🔗 [DirtyWarez](https://dirtywarez.org/)** — Video, audio, books, and comics DDL index.

- **🔗 [WarezForums](https://warezforums.com/)** — Community DDL forum covering video, audio, ROMs, books, and comics.

## 🔍 DDL Search Engines

Search across multiple DDL sources at once.

- **🏆 [Download CSE](https://cse.google.com/cse?cx=006516753008110874046:ohkk8gmukdu)** — Google custom search engine scoped to DDL sites. Often finds direct links fast.

- **💎 [FilePursuit](https://filepursuit.com/)** — Indexes open FTP and HTTP file servers. Surprisingly effective.

- **🔗 [Napalm FTP](https://www.searchftps.net/)** — FTP server search. Good for finding obscure files.

- **🔗 [Mamont](https://www.mmnt.ru/)** — FTP search engine. Use with a translator.

## 🌊 Torrenting

### Torrent Clients

:::warning
Bind your VPN to your torrent client to prevent IP leaks. Most clients support this natively.
:::

- **🏆 [qBittorrent](https://www.qbittorrent.org/)** — The best free, open source torrent client. No ads, no bloat, feature-rich. Available on all platforms.

- **💎 [Deluge](https://deluge-torrent.org/)** — Lightweight, open source, highly extensible via plugins. Great for headless/server setups.

- **💎 [Transmission](https://transmissionbt.com/)** — Minimal, fast, and open source. The go-to client on Linux and macOS.

- **🔗 [rTorrent](https://github.com/rakshasa/rtorrent)** — Powerful CLI torrent client. Pair with [Flood](https://flood.js.org/) or RuTorrent for a web UI.

- **🔗 [BiglyBT](https://www.biglybt.com/)** — Feature-packed open source client. Supports I2P torrenting for extra privacy.

### Torrent Sites

- **🏆 [1337x](https://1337x.to/)** — Large, well-organised torrent site. Video, audio, games, software. Has mirrors.

- **💎 [RuTracker](https://rutracker.org/)** — Russian tracker with an enormous library including rare content. Requires free sign-up. Use with a translator.

- **💎 [RARBG Dump](https://rarbggo.org/)** — Archive of the now-defunct RARBG. Video, audio, games, books.

- **💎 [LimeTorrents](https://www.limetorrents.lol/)** — Clean torrent index. Video, audio, books.

- **🔗 [TorrentDownloads](https://www.torrentdownloads.pro/)** — General torrent index. Video, audio, books.

### Torrent Aggregators

Search across multiple trackers at once.

- **🏆 [Knaben](https://knaben.eu/)** — One of the best multi-tracker search engines. Fast and comprehensive.

- **💎 [BTDigg](https://btdig.com/)** — DHT-based torrent search. Finds torrents without relying on tracked indexes.

- **🔗 [TorrentProject](https://torrentproject2.com/)** — DHT-based search engine.

- **🔗 [Torrent CSE](https://cse.google.com/cse?cx=006516753008110874046:--3lqvhkstk)** — Google custom search engine across major torrent sites.

### Private Trackers

Invite-only torrent communities with better speeds, content, and retention than public sites.

- **💎 [r/trackers](https://www.reddit.com/r/trackers/)** — Community discussion on private trackers, invite threads, and news.

- **💎 [OpenSignups](https://opensignups.net/)** — Lists private trackers currently open for registration. No invite needed during open signup windows.

- **🔗 [Private Tracker Spreadsheet](https://hdvinnie.github.io/Private-Tracker-Spreadsheet/)** — Comprehensive comparison of private trackers by category and quality.

- **🔗 [Tracker Pathways](https://rentry.org/private-trackers)** — Guide to progressing through invite tiers across the private tracker ecosystem.

## 🔑 Debrid Services

Debrid services unlock premium download speeds on file hosts and can cache torrents to cloud servers for instant HTTP downloads — bypassing the need to torrent directly.

- **🏆 [Real-Debrid](https://real-debrid.com/)** — The most widely supported debrid service. Unlocks 50+ file hosts and caches torrents. ~€3/mo. Works with JDownloader, Stremio/Torrentio, and many media apps.

- **💎 [TorBox](https://torbox.app/)** — Modern debrid with torrent, Usenet, and web download support. Freemium tier available (10GB/mo). Growing app support.

- **💎 [Premiumize](https://www.premiumize.me/)** — Debrid + cloud storage combo. Supports many file hosts and has a clean file manager. ~€9.99/mo.

- **🔗 [AllDebrid](https://alldebrid.com/)** — Similar to Real-Debrid, supports a wide list of hosts. ~€3/mo.

- **🔗 [Debrid-Link](https://debrid-link.com/)** — French debrid service. Competitive pricing and decent host support.

- **🔗 [Debrid Services Comparison](https://www.reddit.com/r/Piracy/wiki/megathread/tools#wiki_debrid_services)** — Community comparison of all major debrid services.

### Debrid Apps & Managers

- **💎 [Debrid Media Manager](https://debridmediamanager.com/)** — Browser-based UI for browsing, searching, and streaming your Real-Debrid/AllDebrid library.

- **🔗 [Stremio](https://www.stremio.com/) + [Torrentio](https://torrentio.strem.fun/)** — Pair with Real-Debrid for near-Netflix quality streaming directly from cached torrents. No downloading needed.

## ☁️ Usenet

Usenet is a fast, uncapped, and hard-to-monitor alternative to torrenting. Requires a paid provider and indexer.

- **🏆 [Newshosting](https://www.newshosting.com/)** — Popular Usenet provider. Fast speeds, long retention. Free trial available.

- **💎 [SABnzbd](https://sabnzbd.org/)** — The best Usenet downloader. Web UI, automation-friendly, integrates with Sonarr/Radarr. Open source.

- **💎 [NZBGet](https://nzbget.net/)** — Lightweight, fast Usenet downloader. Low resource usage. Open source.

- **💎 [NZBHydra2](https://github.com/theotherp/nzbhydra2)** — Meta search across multiple NZB indexers in one place. Open source.

- **🔗 [r/usenet](https://www.reddit.com/r/usenet/)** — Usenet community. Provider deals, indexer recommendations, setup guides.

## 🤖 Download Automation

Set up fully automated media libraries — searches, downloads, and organises everything for you.

- **🏆 [Sonarr](https://sonarr.tv/)** — Automatic TV show downloader and library manager. Works with torrents, Usenet, and debrids. Open source.

- **🏆 [Radarr](https://radarr.video/)** — Same as Sonarr but for movies. Open source.

- **💎 [Prowlarr](https://github.com/Prowlarr/Prowlarr)** — Indexer manager for Sonarr/Radarr. Manages all your torrent and Usenet indexers in one place.

- **💎 [Lidarr](https://lidarr.audio/)** — Automated music downloader and library manager. Open source.

- **🔗 [Readarr](https://readarr.com/)** — Automated ebook and audiobook downloader. Open source.

- **🔗 [Bazarr](https://www.bazarr.media/)** — Automatic subtitle downloader for Sonarr and Radarr libraries.

:::info
Sonarr + Radarr + Prowlarr + Jellyfin + Real-Debrid is one of the most popular setups for a free, self-hosted streaming experience.
:::

## 🛡️ Safety & Tools

- **🏆 [uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/)** — Essential for DDL and torrent sites. Blocks malicious ads and pop-ups. Available for Firefox and Chromium browsers.

- **💎 [VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com/)** — Scan files and URLs against 70+ antivirus engines before running anything.

- **💎 [Triage](https://tria.ge/)** — Online sandbox for running suspicious files safely. More thorough than VirusTotal.

- **💎 [T2M](https://torrent2magnet.com/)** — Convert torrent files to magnet links without opening a client.

- **🔗 [IKnowWhatYouDownload](https://iknowwhatyoudownload.com/)** — Check what torrents are publicly associated with your IP. Good motivation to use a VPN.

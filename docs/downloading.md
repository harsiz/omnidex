# Downloading

DDL sites, torrent clients, download managers, debrids, and other tools 

:::info Trust Rating Emojis

    **"💎"** indicates recommended and trusted source.
    **"🏆"** indicates the best pick in the author(s) opinion.

Always use an **ad blocker** on DDL and torrent sites. Use a **VPN** when torrenting to prevent your ISP throttling your Internet speeds (they will do this without warning or informing you of this).
:::

:::warning
It's prudent to scan downloaded software with [VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com/) before installing.
:::

### Use these when torrenting

- **🏆 [uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/)** - Absolutely essential. The free version works just as well as the paid.

- **💎 [VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com/)** - Scan files and URLs against antivirus engines before running them.

- **💎 [Triage](https://tria.ge/)** - Online sandbox for running suspicous files where they cant harm your device.
